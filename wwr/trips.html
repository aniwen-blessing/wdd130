Salmon River Adventure

Snake River Scenic Float

Class I-II Half Day $89

Relax on a peaceful float trip through stunning canyon scenery. Perfect for families and first-time rafters who want to enjoy nature without the intensity of big rapids.

  • Season: April - October

Class IV-V 2 Days $299

Challenge yourself on the exciting Class IV-V rapids of the Payette River. This multi-day expedition includes riverside camping and is perfect for experienced rafters seeking the ultimate adventure.

  • Season: June - August
